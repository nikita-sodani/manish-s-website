About

Bridging Elite Sport & Advanced Rehabilitation

I'm Manish Kumar Singh — Sports Physiotherapist and founder of Back to Function.

My practice is built on one core belief: recovery should bring you back to movement faster, and the foundation you build during that process should last a lifetime. I work with each person to not just heal what's hurting, but to strengthen and stabilise what surrounds it — so you come back stronger than before.

With experience at the highest levels of sport — from the ATP and WTA tours to Pro Kabaddi, Davis Cup, and beyond — I bring an elite, evidence-based approach to everyone who walks through the door. Because everyone deserves to function at their best.

Expertise

Areas of
Specialization

A comprehensive clinical skill set built across 15 years of elite sport, high-performance practice, and advanced international training.

01

Manipulative Physiotherapy

Advanced hands-on joint and soft tissue mobilization techniques to restore optimal movement and reduce pain in high-performance athletes.

02

Injury Management & Rehabilitation

Comprehensive sports injury assessment and progressive return-to-play programs tailored to each athlete's sport, position, and competitive schedule.

03

Deep Tissue & Recovery Massage

Targeted deep tissue therapy and event recovery massage to accelerate healing, reduce delayed-onset muscle soreness, and maintain peak performance across tournaments.

04

Dry Needling

Precision trigger point dry needling to release myofascial tension, address chronic pain patterns, and restore muscle function in elite athletes.

05

Cupping Therapy

Traditional and modern cupping for enhanced circulation, fascial decompression, and accelerated recovery between competition days.

06

Dynamic Taping

Sport-specific taping strategies to support joints, modify load and biomechanics, and enable athletes to train and compete safely through minor injury.

07

Sport-Specific Fitness & Conditioning

Designing and implementing evidence-based strength, conditioning, and sport-specific fitness programs — from load management during season to off-season rebuilds.

08

Structural Integration

GlobalSI-certified whole-body fascial realignment work to improve posture, movement efficiency, and long-term structural resilience in competitive athletes.
